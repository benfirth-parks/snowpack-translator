Open in Messages
  1. Say the weather and conditions into the box at the top, then tap Add.
  2. Tap Open in Messages. The text is waiting in a new message.
  3. With no cell or Wi-Fi, Messages offers to connect by satellite (iPhone 14 or later, iOS 18 or later). Follow its prompt to point at the sky, then send.

Codes follow Canadian observation shorthand: Sky CLR/FEW/SCT/BKN/OVC/X, Pcp NIL/S1/S2/RL, Wind C/L/M/S/X and direction, Ta/Tx/Tn air temperatures, HS, HN24, HST, PF/PS foot and ski penetration, BS blowing snow. Short texts send fastest by satellite; one text holds 160 characters.
